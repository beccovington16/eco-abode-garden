# Eco.Abode Open Garden

A phone-friendly website visitors use while walking around Eco.Abode during the Castlemaine Festival of Gardens open garden.

## Language

**Stop**:
A physical place in the garden that visitors can see, shown on the Map and marked with its own QR sign.
_Avoid_: Area, section, bed, station

**Feature**:
A story about the property that isn't tied to one spot visitors walk to (e.g. Trust for Nature, the fires).
_Avoid_: Other features, extras

**Map**:
The illustrated plan of the garden where each Stop is numbered and can be tapped.
_Avoid_: Site plan, layout

**Walking order**:
The sequence visitors follow from the gate: Entrance Gardens, Middle Garden, Main Garden, Northern Gardens, Tiered Garden, Water Tank Art, Off-Grid, Rock Garden, Lexie's Run. A Stop's number is its place in this order.
_Avoid_: Route, tour

**Before photo**:
A photo of a Stop from when Shaun and Bec took the property on, shown so visitors can compare it with what's in front of them.
_Avoid_: Old photo, original

**Now photo**:
A recent photo of a Stop, used as a reference so visitors know they're looking at the right spot.
_Avoid_: After photo, current photo

**QR sign**:
A printed sign at a Stop whose code opens that Stop's page.
_Avoid_: Label, tag

## Plants

**Key Plant**:
A plant that grows across several Stops and that visitors often ask about.
_Avoid_: Common plant, feature plant, hero plant

**Plant Guide**:
The single page that describes every Key Plant with a photo.
_Avoid_: Plant list, plant directory
