The biggest change on this side has been the meandering pathway, Lexie's Run, that leads between the entrance and the rock garden.

It's called Lexie's Run because that's exactly what Lexie does: every time a car comes down the driveway, she races down this side to check it out. It's her spot. That gave us the idea for a pathway here, turning what was scrubby bush (like the rest of the land around the driveway) into a little magical garden.

It also plays a starring role in our annual Easter egg hunt with the nieces and nephews.
