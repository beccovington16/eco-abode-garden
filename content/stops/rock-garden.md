Before the bunker went in, this side of the house was tiered and ramped with natives. It was one of the messiest, most overgrown areas of the garden. Shaun was determined to save the native shrubs, which heavily shaped the placement and design of the bunker and the garden around it.

Because we could fill these beds with high-quality soil, they've taken off much faster than the shallower beds where we've had to work with what was already there.
