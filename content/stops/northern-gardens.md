The northern garden sits next to the veggie patch. The smaller bed in front of the carport used to be packed with agapanthus and saltbush.

If you've ever tried to dig out agapanthus, you'll know it's back-breaking work. Our top tip? Get someone else to do it! Someone came along, pulled out the lot and took them home for their own garden, so even our agapanthus got a second life.

This is also where we met our first bearded dragon neighbour. The lizards love the Acacia 'Mini Cogs', which make cosy little homes for them, especially over winter.
