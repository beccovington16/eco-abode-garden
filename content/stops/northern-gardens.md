The northern garden sits next to the veggie patch. The smaller bed in front of the carport used to be full of agapanthus and saltbush, which Lexie loved all too much, flushing out anything that lived there.

For the most part she's now learnt to leave them alone. But if you come across a bush that looks flatter than it should, you know why!
