These gardens have had the biggest transformation on the property. If you came to our last open garden two years ago, you'll see just how far they've come. Back then the beds were brand new, with plenty of bare sand mulch still showing. It certainly wasn't this lush and green!

Take a close look at the Acacia pendula in the before photo to see how much it's grown in five years.

When we arrived, this area was the epitome of cottage garden: bed after bed full of bulbs and succulents. For the first couple of years we really enjoyed watching the colours change through the seasons, but it wasn't the right fit for us and our desire to create a space to enjoy with friends and family.

Those bulbs haven't gone quietly, though. Every spring they rise from the dead like tiny garden zombies, and we still pull out around a hundred tulips a year. So if you spot the odd bulb poking up between the natives, we're working on it!
