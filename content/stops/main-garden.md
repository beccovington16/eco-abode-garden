These gardens have had the biggest transformation on the property, and much of it was done quite recently. It's only a baby.

When we arrived, this area was the epitome of cottage garden: bed after bed full of bulbs and succulents. For the first couple of years we really enjoyed it, watching the colours change through the seasons. But it wasn't the right fit for us and our desire to create a space to enjoy with friends and family.

Please enjoy it while it's young. We can't wait for it to take off.
