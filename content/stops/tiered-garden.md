The top tier of these beds were the first we transformed, and you can really see them starting to take shape.

The view from the verandah over the tiered beds is one of our favourite places. The stone retaining walls and steps were already here, as were many of the larger natives. It was also here that Bec found her love of bird watching, with the pollinating plants inviting an abundance of birdlife.

Stop for a moment, take a seat on the verandah and soak up the view of Leanganook (Mt Alexander) and the Harcourt Valley.
