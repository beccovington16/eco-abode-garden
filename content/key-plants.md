## Kangaroo Paw
botanical: Anigozanthos
photo:

Fuzzy, claw-shaped flowers that the birds love. Tough once established and flowers for months.

## Lomandra
botanical: Lomandra longifolia
photo:

A hardy, grassy tussock that handles dry spells and gives structure all year round.

## Myoporum
botanical: Myoporum parvifolium
photo:

A low, spreading groundcover that knits the beds together. You'll see it everywhere.

## Eremophila
botanical: Eremophila glabra
photo:

Also called emu bush. Tubular flowers that feed honeyeaters, on tough silvery or green foliage.

## Grevillea
botanical: Grevillea
photo:

Spidery flowers loved by birds and bees, from groundcovers to big shrubs.

## Correa
botanical: Correa
photo:

Native fuchsia. Little bell-shaped flowers through the cooler months when not much else is flowering.

## Banksia
botanical: Banksia spinulosa and Banksia ericifolia
photo:

Big candle-shaped flower spikes in autumn and winter that feed honeyeaters.

## Acacia 'Mini Cog'
botanical: Acacia cognata 'Mini Cog'
photo:

A soft, weeping little wattle that forms a neat mound. Great for movement in the wind.

## Pigface
botanical: Carpobrotus
photo:

A succulent groundcover with bright pink daisy-like flowers that copes with heat and poor soil.

## Diggers Speedwell
botanical: Veronica perfoliata
photo:

Blue-grey foliage with sprays of violet-blue flowers in spring.

## Hop Bush
botanical: Dodonaea viscosa
photo:

A fast-growing shrub with papery, hop-like seed capsules that turn red and purple.
