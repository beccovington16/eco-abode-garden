---
title: Why sand mulch?
---
It's the question we get asked most (after "what's that plant?"). A few reasons:

- **It won't burn.** Unlike bark or woodchips, sand can't catch fire, so it's part of our fire defence around the house.
- **Natives love it.** Lots of our plants come from sandy, free-draining country and hate wet feet. Sand keeps water away from their stems and helps stop rot.
- **It reflects the sun.** The pale sand bounces light back up into the plants, which our natives enjoy, and it stops the soil underneath from heating up.
- **Fewer weeds.** Weed seeds struggle to take hold in it, and the ones that do pull out easily.
- **It looks great.** The pale sand makes the silvery and grey foliage really stand out.
