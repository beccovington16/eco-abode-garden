We're Shaun, Bec and Lexie the kelpie.

Eco.Abode entered our life in 2021. Fate (and a pandemic) propelled us toward our retirement dream: an off-grid bush paradise.

The garden is our escape from the daily grind. It's also soaked up a huge amount of our time, especially Shaun's. After visiting gardens in the 2022 Festival of Gardens, Shaun set an ambitious goal to take part.

This is actually the garden's second life in the public eye. Back in 2000 it was featured in Australian Country Style magazine as a beautiful cottage garden, lovingly developed over many years by the previous owner, who still pops back to visit quite often.

When we arrived, while the bones were good, the garden was in desperate need of some TLC. Shaun set to work, intent on building his dream native garden, and nothing went to waste along the way. He offered what came out as swaps, so plenty of the old cottage garden now lives on in gardens around Castlemaine. In return, people brought us natives, honey and eggs. Not a bad trade!

Neither of us had gardening experience, so we're on a roller coaster of learning. We're incredibly grateful to the local folks who've given us great advice on helping natives thrive in Castlemaine's challenging soil.

The gardens are inspired by naturalists Gordon Ford and Sam Cox, the coastal garden styles of Peter Shaw and the forms of Fiona Brockhoff. You'll notice a lot of repeat planting weaving through all the gardens, connecting them.
