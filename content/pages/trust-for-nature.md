In Victoria, 62% of land is privately owned, so working with private landholders is critical to securing the future of our unique ecosystems and species.

Landowners can protect native habitat through a conservation covenant: a voluntary, legal agreement that permanently protects the natural or cultural values of the land. Trust for Nature has protected more than 110,000 hectares of private land in Victoria, and our 30 acres is part of that.

The previous owners did the lion's share of the work to protect the property, and our role now is maintenance. That mainly means keeping the weeds out (ask Shaun about his fight with the thistles!) and reducing erosion in the gullies.
